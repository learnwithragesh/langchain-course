# Sweet Crumbs Bakery: Employee Handbook

Sweet Crumbs is a fictional bakery chain founded in 2011 by Lakshmi Narayanan in Coimbatore.
It now operates 14 stores across Tamil Nadu and Kerala. This handbook is sample data for the LangChain course.

## Working Hours

Stores open at 6:30 AM and close at 9:00 PM every day except Tuesday, when they close at 5:00 PM for deep cleaning.
Baking staff start their shift at 4:30 AM. Counter staff work in two shifts: 6:15 AM to 2:15 PM and 1:00 PM to 9:15 PM.
Overtime must be approved in advance by the store manager and is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate.

## Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per year, plus 12 public holidays.
Leave requests must be submitted in the StaffHub app at least 10 days in advance.
Sick leave is 8 days per year; more than 2 consecutive sick days require a doctor's certificate.
Unused annual leave up to 6 days can be carried over to the next year. The rest expires on 31 March.

## Employee Discount

All employees get a 30% discount on bakery products, and 50% on items left unsold at closing time.
The discount is not valid on custom celebration cakes or on catering orders.
Family members are not eligible for the employee discount.

## Uniform and Hygiene

Staff must wear the maroon Sweet Crumbs apron and a hair net at all times in the kitchen.
Hands must be washed for at least 20 seconds before handling food. Nail polish and jewellery (except a plain wedding band) are not allowed in the kitchen.
Uniforms are provided free of charge: three aprons per employee per year.

## Training

New employees complete a 5-day onboarding programme called "First Rise".
It covers food safety, the POS system, and the brand story. Bakers additionally complete a 3-week apprenticeship with a senior baker.
The annual "Golden Whisk" award goes to the employee with the best customer feedback, and comes with a prize of ₹25,000.

## Complaints and Escalation

Customer complaints should first be handled by the shift lead.
If unresolved, escalate to the store manager within 24 hours, and then to the regional quality team at quality@sweetcrumbs.example.
Food-safety incidents must be reported immediately to the regional manager by phone.
